# AGAP modern pixel-art assets

These newly generated PNGs use contemporary emergency-assistance imagery and have no connection to the previous campaign.

- `agap-character-atlas.png` — 1536 × 1024 transparent PNG with three equal-width sprite columns (resident, volunteer, emergency responder). Each column can be animated separately without flattening the alpha channel.
- `agap-action-atlas.png` — matching alternate action poses: tapping the SOS, receiving a message and stepping forward to respond. Same 1536 × 1024 transparent layout, used with the original to animate character actions.
- `storm-city.png` — wide pixel-art environment plate, a generic modern Filipino neighborhood during rainfall; designed for animated characters and signal overlays.
- [story/](story/) — seven sequential action sprite sheets (44 individually drawn character frames), two new environments, and illustrated phone/hand close-ups used in the narrative film.

## Generation prompts

The assets were created with the built-in image generator:

**Character atlas:** Create an independent, modern emergency-assistance brand identity using a transparent, three-column animation-ready 16/32-bit pixel-art atlas. A Filipino woman resident with terracotta rain jacket and smartphone; a Filipino man volunteer with teal jacket and phone; a Filipino woman emergency responder with a teal/slate EMS uniform, medical bag and radio. Full-body, aligned, individually isolated, premium pixel shading, crisp square pixel clusters, no text, logos, folklore, costumes or festival imagery.

**Environment:** Wide cinematic pixel-art modern Filipino urban neighborhood during a rainstorm at dusk, low-rise buildings, modern utility poles and distant communications mast, amber windows, wet road and foreground space for separate actors. Deep slate/teal and muted blues, restrained amber and terracotta accents, crisp 16/32-bit pixel clusters; no people, landmarks, festival imagery, text or logos.

**Alternate poses:** Preserve the original atlas exactly in identity, proportions, palette and sprite layout. Change only poses: resident focused and tapping the phone, volunteer checking the phone with both hands, responder taking a forward step with medical bag and radio. Separate full-body transparent cutouts, same pixel-art treatment.
